### Title 
Payroll can be generated before employee joining date

## Severity
High

## Priority
High

## Description
The system allows payroll generation for periods before the employee's joining date.



## Steps to Reproduce

1. Launch the application.
2. Login as HR/Admin.
3. Click on Employees from the left navigation bar.
4. click on Add Employee icon.
5. Fill all the required details.
6. Create on Create Employee.
7. Click on Payroll from the left navigation Bar.
8. Click on generate.
9. Select the month May and current year and Click on generate.
10. it is observed that payroll still showing the same amount as the previous month.

## Expected Result

Payroll should not be generated for any period before the employee's joining date.

## Actual Result

Payroll is generated successfully even though the employee was not employed during that period.

## Impact

This creates incorrect payroll records and may result in payment calculations for periods when the employee was not working for the company.

## Possible Root Cause

Payroll generation may not be validating employee joining date before calculating salary.
