# Title

Approved leave / unpaid leave is not deducted during payroll generation

## Severity
High

## Priority
High

## Description

Even after a leave request is approved, payroll generation does not consider the approved leave information correctly.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Launch the application.
2. Login as Employee with email: emily.d@acme.com & Password : Employee@123.
3. Click on Leave from the left Navigation Bar.
4. Click On Apply Leave.
5. Enter All the details and Click on Submit Application.
6. Logout as Employee.
7. Login as Hr/Admin.
8. Click on Leave from the left Navigation Bar.
9. Select and Approve the leave.
10. Click on Payroll from the left navigation Bar.
11. Click on generate.
12. Select the valid month and year and Click on generate.
13. it is observed that payroll showing the amount without calculating the leaves.


## Expected Result

Payroll calculation should correctly consider approved leave records according to business rules.

## Actual Result

Payroll is generated without considering the approved leave information.

## Impact

Employees may receive incorrect salary calculations, leading to payroll disputes and manual corrections.

## Possible Root Cause

Payroll calculation module may not be fetching approved leave records during salary processing.
