### Title 

Tax changes not reflecting in the payroll generation

## Severity

Critical

## Priority

High

## Description

After changing the tax percentage from the Admin or HR profile, it is observed that the updated tax percentage is not reflected during payroll generation.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. launch the browser.
2. Enter the Url :
3. Login with email: admin@payroll.com & password: Admin@123 .
4. Click on Settings in the left navigation Bar.
5. Change the tax % Value to 20 .
6. Click on Save.
7. Click on Payroll from the left navigation Bar.
8. Click on generate.
9. Select the valid month and year and Click on generate.
10. it is observed that payroll still showing the same amount as the previous month.

## Expected Result

Payroll should be generated accoring tax %  configured by Admin or HR .


## Actual Result

The updated tax percentage is not reflected in payroll generation


## Impact

Incorrect payroll may be calculated for employees, resulting in inaccurate salary deductions and financial discrepancies.

## Possible Root Cause

Payroll calculation may be using a default or previously cached tax percentage instead of the latest configured value.
Payroll generation may not be fetching the updated tax configuration during salary calculation
